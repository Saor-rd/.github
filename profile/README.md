# Saor R&D — Software Engineering & Applied Research

*The ambition of an institution. The agility of an individual.*

Saor R&D is a small, independent applied research laboratory. We explore questions at the intersection of software engineering, systems design, and practical experimentation—especially where privacy, verification, and human control matter.

We are interested in ideas that can be made concrete, challenged, measured, and improved.

## How we work

Our work begins with a question, not a pitch.

We make assumptions explicit, build prototypes, design experiments, preserve evidence, and change direction when the results disagree with us. We distinguish promising ideas from demonstrated results, and experimental systems from software that is ready for wider use.

AI-assisted engineering is one part of our working method. It expands what a small laboratory can investigate, but it does not replace human responsibility, technical judgment, or independent verification.

**Lead with purpose. Work with autonomy. Challenge with evidence. Judge by results.**

## What we investigate

Our interests include:

- coordination and control systems;
- verification and execution boundaries;
- privacy-oriented communication;
- local-first and user-controlled software;
- engineering history and decision provenance;
- tools that help people understand and control the systems they use.

These areas are connected by a common question: how can software remain capable without taking understanding or control away from its users?

## Selected work

**TPMS** is an experimental software control-plane system concerned with coordination, verification, execution boundaries, and evidence-aware engineering work.

**The Commons** is a long-term exploration of privacy-oriented digital community and communication infrastructure.

Other work includes AHRN research and neutral experimental infrastructure associated with TPMS. These projects remain experimental unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Following the work

We will publish repositories, technical notes, and research results selectively when they are ready for responsible disclosure.

Unfinished research, security-sensitive material, and private development history remain private by default. Publication is a deliberate engineering decision, not an automatic by-product of the work.
